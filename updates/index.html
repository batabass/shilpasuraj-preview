As their battle for power intensifies, Ishaan seeks revenge, determined to take everything from her, including her father’s prized possession: her. Dr. Amay Aatre, a stoic trauma surgeon, is haunted by his past and his mother’s death. When Dhrithi Sahay, a survivor of domestic abuse and his lost love, lands in his ER, old wounds reopen. As Amay fights to save her, police investigations reveal dangerous secrets from her past. Amay must confront his emotions and decide if he’s willing to risk his heart to protect the woman he still loves.

Beyond The Box
